    About us

    Real estate development from Saida, for over 69 years.

    69 years of experience in real estate development

    Serop Kizirian Development builds lifestyle luxury villas in and around Saida. We take care of each villa from A to Z: the land, the build, the materials and the finishes you choose with our interior designers.

    We work from two offices, our main office on Baasiri Street in Saida and a second office in Chehime. Every project is open for site visits, so you can walk the land and see the build before you decide.

    How your villa comes together

    1. Visit the site

      Walk the land and the villas with our team. Book a visit at a time that suits you.

    2. Choose your villa

      Pick the project, the plot and the villa that fit your family.

    3. Shape the finishes

      Choose materials, floors, kitchen and lighting with our interior designers.

    4. Pay over two years

      A flexible payment plan spread across two years while we build.
